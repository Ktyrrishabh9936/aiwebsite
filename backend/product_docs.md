# Getting started with Arevei
Keywords: start, setup, onboarding, first steps, next, recommend, benefit, value, overview, what is arevei
## What it does
Arevei is an AI sales team for your business. It captures leads from ads and landing pages, calls them within minutes to qualify them, keeps your CRM organised, and gives you a daily plan to hit your revenue target.
## Why it matters
Most businesses lose leads to slow follow-up and no clear plan. Arevei fixes both automatically.
## How to set it up
1. Train your Business Brain with your website.
2. Answer the AI-guided sales strategy questions.
3. Connect a voice agent so new leads are called automatically.
4. Connect lead sources: Ads to CRM and Landing Page Forms (Workflows).
5. Check the AI Manager's morning briefing each day.
## Tips to get the most out of it
- Connect at least one lead source in your first week; the AI gets better with real leads.
- Need help? The Arevei team can set everything up for you: contact arevei at admin@arevei.com.
## Questions people ask
- How long does setup take? About 8–10 minutes with the AI-guided setup.
- Can someone set it up for me? Yes. Choose "Contact Arevei for setup" or email admin@arevei.com.

# AI Manager
Keywords: what can you do, capabilities, help, manager, assistant, agent mode, chat, sales head
## What it does
The AI Manager is your always-on sales head. It reads your CRM, Business Brain and sales strategy, answers questions about your pipeline, and carries out internal work for you: adding and updating leads, saving notes, scheduling human follow-ups, assigning salespeople and starting initial AI qualification calls.
## Why it matters
Instead of digging through screens, you ask in plain language ("Which hot leads haven't been contacted this week?") and get an answer grounded in your real data. Agent Mode can do multi-step work such as reviewing every stale lead and scheduling follow-ups.
## How to set it up
1. Finish onboarding so the Manager knows your business (Brain) and goals (Sales strategy).
2. Open the chat bubble at the bottom right of any workspace page, or open AI Manager in the sidebar.
3. Switch on Agent Mode when you want the Manager to take actions, not just answer.
## Tips to get the most out of it
- Ask for a morning briefing: "What should my team focus on today?"
- Report outcomes after calls: "Priya agreed to a site visit on Friday, add a note and a follow-up."
- Keep your sales strategy up to date so advice matches your current targets.
## Questions people ask
- What can you do? Answer questions about leads, payments, follow-ups and strategy; create and update leads; schedule follow-ups; assign salespeople; start initial qualification calls; explain any Arevei feature.
- Can it message customers? No. It never sends messages, publishes, deletes or takes payments on its own.

# Business Brain
Keywords: brain, website, crawl, train, retrain, company knowledge, business profile
## What it does
The Brain is the AI's understanding of your company. Arevei reads your website and builds a profile of your business, offers, audience, brand voice, goals and evidence. Every AI feature uses it.
## Why it matters
A trained Brain makes qualification calls, strategy and Manager answers sound like your business instead of generic AI.
## How to set it up
1. Add your website during onboarding (or create a workspace with your website URL).
2. Training runs in the background and usually finishes in a few minutes.
3. Review and edit it in Brain → Company knowledge. Use Retrain after big website changes.
## Tips to get the most out of it
- Correct anything wrong in the Brain; the AI treats it as truth.
- Add details your website doesn't show, such as pricing ranges or service areas.
## Questions people ask
- My website blocks crawlers or I don't have one. Answer the onboarding questions; Arevei builds a starter Brain from your answers.

# Sales strategy and daily briefings
Keywords: strategy, roadmap, revenue target, briefing, morning review, goals, plan, budget
## What it does
From your offers, ideal customer, team capacity, budget and revenue target, the AI Manager writes a month-by-month sales strategy and creates priority tasks. Every morning it reviews your pipeline and sends a briefing with overdue work and stale leads.
## Why it matters
You get a clear plan tied to your revenue goal and a daily nudge on what moves the number, without hiring a full-time sales manager.
## How to set it up
1. Answer the AI-guided setup questions during onboarding, or open Brain → Sales strategy.
2. Click Build my sales strategy.
3. Set your timezone and morning briefing hour so briefings arrive at the right time.
## Tips to get the most out of it
- Revisit the strategy monthly or when your target changes.
- Be honest about team capacity; the plan sizes the work to it.
## Questions people ask
- Can I skip the questions? Yes. Every question can be skipped, but the strategy needs at least your business, offers, ideal customer, team and revenue target.

# CRM and pipeline

## What it does
The CRM holds every lead with custom fields, pipeline stages, tags, notes, follow-up reminders, qualification results, payment plans, receipts and invoices.

## Questions people ask

# AI lead qualification
Keywords: qualification, qualify, ai call, calling, hot, warm, cold, junk, questions, speed to lead
## What it does
When a new lead arrives, an AI voice agent calls them, asks your qualification questions, and records a summary, transcript, score and category (hot, warm, cold or junk) on the lead.
## Why it matters
Speed to lead wins deals. Leads are contacted within minutes, day or night, and your team only spends time on qualified buyers.
## How to set it up
1. Connect a voice provider (see Voice agent).
2. Open Qualification to set your questions and calling mode: automatic, scheduled or manual.
3. New leads with the AI call enabled are called according to those settings.
## Tips to get the most out of it
- Keep qualification questions to 4–6 short ones.
- Phone numbers must include the country code (for example +91) so calls can be placed.
## Questions people ask
- Can I turn off the AI call for a lead? Yes. Untick "Trigger AI call" when adding the lead, or in a landing page form's lead defaults.

# Voice agent
Keywords: voice, call, phone, plivo, sarvam, telephony, calling number, inbound
## What it does
The voice agent places AI qualification calls to new leads and can answer inbound calls. Arevei supports Plivo and Sarvam as voice providers.
## Why it matters
An AI that calls every lead instantly increases contact rates and frees your team from first-touch calls.
## How to set it up
1. During onboarding open Voice agent, or go to Settings → Voice providers.
2. Choose Plivo or Sarvam and enter your account details and calling number.
3. Click Test to confirm, then enable it.
## Tips to get the most out of it
- Use a local number for the country you're calling; answer rates are higher.
- Use the Sarvam setup guide to generate an agent prompt from your business details.
## Questions people ask
- I don't have a provider account. Contact arevei at admin@arevei.com and the team will help set it up.

# SMS
Keywords: sms, text message, twilio, texting
## What it does
Send SMS to leads directly from their CRM record using your Twilio account. Messages are logged on the lead.
## Why it matters
Some leads never answer calls but reply to texts. SMS gives your team a second channel without leaving Arevei.
## How to set it up
1. During onboarding open SMS, or go to Settings → SMS (Twilio).
2. Enter your Twilio Account SID, Auth Token and sending number or Messaging Service SID.
3. Enable SMS and save.
## Tips to get the most out of it
- Keep messages short and personal; mention what the lead asked about.
## Questions people ask
- Does the AI send SMS automatically? No. SMS is sent by your team from the lead record.

# Ads to CRM (Meta Lead Ads via Google Sheets)
Keywords: meta, facebook, instagram, lead ads, google sheet, ads, workflow, connector
## What it does
Imports Meta (Facebook and Instagram) lead ad responses into the CRM through a Google Sheet, including campaign, ad set, ad and form attribution.
## Why it matters
Ad leads are contacted within minutes instead of sitting in a spreadsheet, and you can see which campaigns produce real buyers.

1. Open Workflows → Ads to CRM.
2. Connect your Google account and choose the spreadsheet and tab your Meta leads go to.

## Questions people ask

# Landing Page Forms
Keywords: landing page, website form, form, embed, utm, webflow, wordpress, elementor, framer, webhook, zapier, capture leads, website leads
## What it does
Sends leads from any website or landing page straight into the CRM. Each form captures name, email, phone with country code, the visitor's requirement, and hidden UTM tracking (source, medium, campaign, term, content) so you know which campaign produced each lead.
## Why it matters
Website leads get the same instant AI qualification call as ad leads, and UTM tracking shows which campaigns and pages actually bring buyers.
## How to set it up
1. Open Workflows → Landing Page Forms and click New form.
2. Choose which fields to show and which are required, and set lead defaults (source label, stage, tags, AI call).
3. Click Share & embed. Copy the hosted form link, use the responsive iframe, connect an existing form, or open the advanced API option.
4. Add your website domain under Security and access, save the form, then send a test lead.
## Tips to get the most out of it
- Use a separate form per landing page or campaign so you can compare results.
- Tag ad links with utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign; Arevei captures them automatically.
- The hosted link and responsive iframe both preserve UTM and click-ID attribution.
- Set the default country code to where most of your customers are.
## Questions people ask
- What if the same person submits twice? Arevei updates the existing lead and notes the repeat enquiry instead of creating a duplicate.
- Does it work with WordPress or Webflow? Yes. Use "Connect existing form" or the embed script.
- Can I share the form without a website? Yes. Click Share & embed and copy the hosted page link.
